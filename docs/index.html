How it works

  1. IWrite it down. The way you'd say it: Call dentist tomorrow 3pm #Health p2. Telos understands it on your phone.
  2. IIGive it a reason. Name a goal, say why it matters, and link the projects that serve it. One next step stays in view.
  3. IIILook back, gently. Each quarter, a short review asks what moved and what didn't. No streaks, no points, no red.

What it does

Everything a task list needs, and the reason behind it.

  • Inbox, Today, Upcoming And projects, with subprojects, sections, labels, priorities and subtasks. Show a project as a list or a board.
  • Quick add in plain words Dates, times, projects, labels and priorities in one line, understood on your phone. Nothing is sent to be parsed.
  • Recurring tasks “Every weekday”, “3 days after completion” and more, with reminders that come back after a restart.
  • Goals and next steps The why behind your projects. Each goal keeps one concrete next step in view, and a goal that's gone quiet is gently pointed out.
  • A quarterly review A few calm questions every three months: what moved, what didn't, and is this still the right goal?
  • Filters and search Save the views you use, search everything, and change many tasks at once.
  • Quick to reach Home-screen widgets, a Quick Settings tile, app shortcuts, and “Add to Telos” from any app's share sheet or selected text.
  • Earlier, not overdue Tasks from earlier days wait under a neutral heading, in normal ink. Never red, never “you missed”.

Private by design

Your plans are your business.

No account, no analytics, no ads and no servers of our own. Your tasks stay on your phone, and Telos doesn't connect to anything unless you turn on the backup.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, every feature, for everyone. There are no ads or in-app purchases, and the app doesn't contain ad or payment code. If you'd like to help, you can support it on Ko-fi.

Can I bring my tasks from Todoist?

Yes. Export a project from Todoist as a CSV file and import it in Telos, under Settings, Import, export and backup. You can also export everything from Telos at any time, as JSON or CSV.

What's a goal, and how is it different from a project?

A project is a pile of work. A goal is the reason for it, like “Finish the book, because it matters to me”. Link the projects that serve a goal, pin one task as its next step, and Telos keeps that step in view.

What if I delete something by mistake?

It goes to the trash, where it's kept for 30 days. Restore it from there whenever you like.

Do I need an account? What if I change phones?

No account needed. To move to a new phone, restore your encrypted backup from your own Google Drive, or export a file and import it on the new phone.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Telos is Android-only for now (Android 8.0 and later), and it's coming to Google Play soon.

Why “Telos”?

Telos (τέλος) is the Greek word for the end a thing aims at: its purpose. The mark is an open ring around a point, left open because the work is still on its way there.
