**App name** (max 30): `Telos – Tasks & Goals` (family format, docs/family-look.md)

**Full description** (max 4000):

> Telos is a quiet task manager that remembers why. Keep your tasks and projects, and above them
> the goals they serve: one next step for each, and a calm review each quarter.
>
> **Tasks**
> Inbox, Today, Upcoming and Projects, with sections, boards, labels, priorities and subtasks.
> Type it the way you'd say it: "Call dentist tomorrow 3pm #Health p2", understood on your phone.
> Recurring tasks ("every weekday", "3 days after completion") and reminders that come back after
> a restart. Saved filters, search and bulk edits.
>
> **Goals**
> The reason behind your projects. Each goal keeps one next step in view, and a short quarterly
> review asks what moved and what didn't.
>
> **Quick to reach**
> Home-screen widgets, a Quick Settings tile, app shortcuts, and "Add to Telos" from any app's
> share sheet or selected text.
>
> **Private by design**
> • Free, with no ads, no accounts and no analytics
> • Works fully offline; your tasks stay on your phone
> • Optional backup to your own Google Drive, encrypted on your phone with your passphrase
> • Export to JSON or CSV, import from a file
> • App lock with PIN or fingerprint
> • Light and dark themes, large text and TalkBack support
>
> No streaks, no points, no red "overdue". Earlier tasks wait under a neutral heading.
