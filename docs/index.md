# Telos

Tasks with a reason. A private task manager and goal tracker for Android.

- Inbox, Today, Upcoming and Projects, with sections, boards, labels, priorities and subtasks.
- Recurring tasks and reminders that survive a restart.
- Quick add in plain words, understood on your phone: `Call dentist tomorrow 3pm #Health p2`.
- Goals: the reason behind your projects, one next step for each, and a quiet quarterly review.
- Free, with no ads, no accounts and no analytics. Your tasks stay on your phone.
- App lock, export to a file, and an optional encrypted backup to your own Google Drive.
